Kristian Droste

Founder's Journal

Kris built the IntoMind One and the IntoMind Mu models with the help of an AI assistant, giving it instructions and making the decisions in writing as the work went. This journal was written by an AI from the records of that work: their conversations, logs, measurements and commits. The quotations in bold are Kris's own words, written to the AI while the work was happening. To read more of his words, see his own writing.
